# Center platform marks

The Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Telegram and Discourse SVG geometry comes from
[Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons).
Paths are unchanged; explicit fills use platform colors for recognition.
The upstream CC0-1.0 license is retained at
`desktop/src/assets/platforms/simple-icons-LICENSE.md`.
Brand and trademark rights remain with their owners.

The icons are bundled locally, not fetched from platform services at runtime.
Adjacent text supplies the accessible platform name. Owned-site marks reuse
the project's existing assets under `sites/branding`.
